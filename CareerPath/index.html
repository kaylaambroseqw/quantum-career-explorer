Cross-department mobility

Common cross-functional paths at Quantum Workplace

These moves reflect natural skill and experience overlaps across our teams — and are encouraged as part of our career philosophy.

Career Transition Why It Works What's Needed
Sales Development → Account Executive Strong pipeline mastery and deal knowledge 12–24 months of consistent SDR quota achievement
Account Executive → Customer Success Manager Customer relationship skills and product knowledge 1–2 years of AE experience; CS orientation
Customer Success Manager → Account Executive Customer relationship depth and product storytelling 3+ years of CS; demonstrated expansion/growth mindset
Software Developer → Product Manager Technical depth and cross-functional experience 2–3 years of Dev experience; PM training or MBA a plus
Customer Support → Customer Implementation Manager Platform expertise and customer communication 2 years of support experience; project management skills
Insights Analyst → Marketing Analyst / Product Manager Data fluency and business impact orientation 2+ years of analytics; demonstrated business partnership
Marketing Analyst → Revenue Operations Manager Campaign analytics and GTM data familiarity 2–3 years of marketing analytics; CRM proficiency
Product Designer → Lead Researcher (Insights) UX research background and human behavioral interest 3+ years of design/research experience
People Operations Specialist → Employee Experience Manager HR knowledge and engagement program interest 2 years in HR operations; strong communication skills
Data Scientist → Product Manager Analytical rigor and product understanding 2+ years of data science; demonstrated PM interest and skills

Smart data model

What powers it all

The experience is only as good as the data behind it. Here's what the model needs to track.

👤

Roles

  • Title & department
  • Level (Intern → C-Suite)
  • IC or Management track
  • Core skills & competencies
  • Salary band
🔗

Connections

  • From role → to role
  • Move type: ↑ Promotion / ↔ Lateral / ⟺ Cross-dept
  • Difficulty & estimated timeline
  • Within-dept or cross-functional
🧠

Skills

  • Skill name
  • Proficiency level required
  • Advancement milestones
  • Linked learning resources
